# Chapter review manifests

Allowed chapter states are `draft`, `technical-review`, `pedagogical-review`, `copy-edit`, and `verified`. A chapter may be marked `verified` only when technical and pedagogical reviewers are named, their approval dates are present, and findings are resolved. Chapters flagged for specialist review also require that approval.

Reviewers must be independent people. Automated checks and an author self-review do not satisfy these fields.
